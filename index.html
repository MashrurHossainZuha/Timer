About Shri Sanatan Mandir

Rooted in the timeless wisdom of the Vedas and the Bhakti tradition, Shri Sanatan Mandir has been a sanctuary of devotion for our community since 1984. Guided by our resident priests and seva volunteers, the temple is dedicated to preserving sacred rituals and sharing their grace with all who visit.

Through daily worship, scriptural study, and cultural celebrations, we nurture the values of compassion, inclusivity, and collective upliftment. Families, elders, and youth gather here to celebrate festivals, honor life milestones, and engage in seva that supports those in need.

Our Mission

  • Honor Sanatan traditions with authenticity and reverence.
  • Provide inclusive spiritual, cultural, and educational programs.
  • Serve the broader community through seva, outreach, and support.
